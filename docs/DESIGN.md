## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for Vietnamese university students navigating rigorous higher education with the support of an AI learning companion. The emotional core balances academic focus, cognitive clarity, and technological optimism. The tone is non-intimidating, clean, and inspiring—eliminating academic anxiety while maintaining authoritative institutional competence.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Soft Minimalist Modernism** paired with purposeful, distraction-free spatial composition. High-clarity white surfaces float over a delicate lavender-tinted canvas, evoking fresh paper and digital quietude. Vibrancy is applied deliberately: vivid purple accents draw attention strictly to critical learning milestones, active inputs, and primary calls-to-action, avoiding cognitive fatigue during prolonged study and revision sessions.

## Colors

The palette employs an intentional chromatic balance designed for reading-intensive digital coursework and long-duration AI prompting sessions.

- **Primary Canvas (`#F9F9FD`)**: An airy, cool lavender-gray neutral background that softens harsh display glare compared to pure white, establishing a comfortable ambient field.
- **Card & Surface Backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white surfaces sit on top of the canvas to differentiate focal containers and form fields with gentle contrast.
- **Primary Accent (`#5B34E6`)**: A vivid violet-indigo commanding energy and cognitive precision. Active/hover transitions step down into `#4E28D4`.
- **Secondary Accent (`#EDE8FD`)**: A weightless lavender tint applied to tag pills, secondary interaction surfaces, and AI icon containers, preventing visual clutter while maintaining tonal cohesion.
- **Typography & Text Contrast**: Headings resolve in near-black slate (`#111827`) for absolute legibility, body content in charcoal (`#374151`), and supporting metadata/form labels in mid-gray (`#6B7280`).
- **Structural Outlines (`#E5E7EB`)**: Low-contrast borders provide crisp tactile form boundaries without introducing rigid visual noise.

## Typography

The typography standard relies on **Be Vietnam Pro**, specifically designed and optimized for complex Vietnamese diacritics and tone marks (dấu câu). This preserves structural balance, eliminates clipping or unnatural inter-line collisions, and guarantees comfortable cadence across academic literature, complex instructional prompts, and technical terminology.

- **Headings & Display**: Bold, tight leadings and subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em`) give student dashboards and module titles an authoritative, editorial finish.
- **Body & Explanatory Prose**: Ample line-height ratios (1.55 to 1.625) accommodate stacked Vietnamese vowel-accent configurations gracefully without crowding surrounding paragraphs.
- **Form Labels & Captions**: Clean medium weights (`500`) keep metadata legible at reduced sizes on mobile viewport widths.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point harmonic spacing grid, prioritizing breathing room and content isolation over dense data packing.

- **Grid Architecture**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`1200px` max content constraint), reflowing to 8 columns on tablet devices (`768px-1024px`) and 4 columns on mobile viewports (`<768px`).
- **Authentication & Focus Layouts**: Distraction-free single-column architecture centered horizontally and vertically within the canvas viewport. Card containers maintain a maximum width of `440px` to keep eye travel narrow and input fatigue minimal.
- **Rhythm Rules**: Vertical stacks inside interactive surfaces adhere to consistent increments: `0.5rem` between labels and fields, `1rem` between sequential form elements, and `1.5rem` separating logical semantic groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft ambient light rather than harsh drop shadows or dense structural frames.

- **Ambient Elevation Tiers**: Surfaces employ extra-diffused shadows carrying a subtle violet tint (`rgba(91, 52, 230, 0.04)` to `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06)`), blending into the tinted neutral canvas seamlessly.
- **Primary Auth Card**: Uses a dual-layer soft elevation: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(91, 52, 230, 0.03), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`. This creates a floating appearance with crisp surface distinction.
- **Interactive Hover Depth**: Elevated cards and primary pill buttons respond with smooth physical transitions (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), increasing diffuse blur while lifting 1px along the Y-axis.
- **Low-Contrast Perimeter Outlines**: Surfaces and inputs maintain a hairline border (`1px solid #E5E7EB` or `#F3F4F6`), preserving interface legibility across low-contrast displays and outdoor mobile study scenarios.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is friendly, approachable, and distinctly contemporary, employing multi-tiered curvature across different component scales:

- **Large Structural Containers (`24px`)**: Module cards, modal dialogs, and primary auth panels use generous 24px corner radii, imparting a welcoming and calm physical feeling.
- **Interactive Form Inputs & Cards (`14px - 16px`)**: Input controls, dropdowns, contextual study hints, and list containers use a disciplined 14px to 16px border radius.
- **Action Pills (`9999px`)**: Primary action triggers, secondary navigation buttons, status chips, and subject tags are fully pill-shaped. This communicates touch-readiness and dynamic interactivity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill)**: Fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), solid `#5B34E6` background, crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 500`. Default height `48px` with `24px` horizontal padding. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#4E28D4` with subtle elevation. Active press scales down slightly (`0.98`).
- **Secondary Action (Pill)**: Fully rounded (`9999px`), soft lavender background (`#EDE8FD`), `#5B34E6` typography. Hover shifts background to `#E2DAFC`.
- **OAuth / Third-Party (Google)**: Clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`), thin border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`), text color `#111827`, accompanied by the official brand icon. Hover produces a subtle border shift (`#D1D5DB`) and background tint (`#F9FAFB`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height of `48px` to `52px`, `border-radius: 14px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, internal horizontal padding `16px`. Body typography styled at `15px` (`#111827`), placeholder text rendered in `#9CA3AF`.
- **Focus State**: Border switches to `#5B34E6` accompanied by an expanded outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(91, 52, 230, 0.15)`. No harsh browser outlines.
- **Labels & Validation**: Labels sit above inputs at `13px`, `font-weight: 500`, `#374151`. Inline feedback (validation/error) uses `#EF4444` with an accompanying warning icon.

### Chips & Subject Badges
- Built as compact pill containers (`height: 28px` to `32px`), padding `4px 12px`, with an `#EDE8FD` background and `#5B34E6` caption text. Ideal for academic tags (e.g., *Toán cao cấp*, *Triết học Mác - Lênin*, *Kỹ thuật lập trình*).

### Cards & Study Modules
- Clean pure-white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed with `1px solid #F3F4F6` and unified `24px` corner radii. Interior padding standardizes to `24px` (or `20px` on mobile screens).

### AI Study Prompt & Chat Snippets
- AI-generated responses reside within soft-tinted cards (`#F8F7FF`) with a faint indigo-tinted border (`#EBE6FD`). An `#EDE8FD` pill badge containing a miniature purple sparkle icon visually designates AI-assisted reasoning.
